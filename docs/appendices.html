10.1 R packages

Used in setting up time series and preprocessing (some packaged used indirectly) - fpp3 (Hyndman, 2021) - tibble (Müller & Wickham, 2021) - dplyr (Wickham, François, Henry & Müller, 2021) - tidyr (Wickham, 2021) - lubridate (Grolemund & Wickham, 2021) - tsibble (Wang, Cook, & Hyndman, 2021) - tsibbledata (O’Hara-Wild, Hyndman, & Wang, 2021d) - feasts (O’Hara-Wild, Hyndman, & Wang, 2021c) - fable (O’Hara-Wild, Hyndman, & Wang, 2021a) - fabletools (O’Hara-Wild, Hyndman, & Wang, 2021b) Bayesian fitting and simulations - bayesforecast (Alonzo & Cruz, 2020) - forecast (Athanasopoulos et al., 2021) Saving data - writexl (Ooms, 2021)
